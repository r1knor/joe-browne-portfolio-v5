# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creative directors, studio managers, recruiters, agencies, and prospective clients evaluating Joe Browne for graphic design, studio artwork, production, UI, branding, signage, and art-direction work; music listeners and collaborators discovering Riknor are the secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

Present Joe's professional design practice and Riknor music work as one coherent creative identity. Success means a visitor can quickly understand Joe's range, inspect real work at useful scale, verify experience and capabilities, and make contact without the experimental presentation becoming difficult to navigate.

## Brand Personality

Precise, nocturnal, tactile. The experience should feel cinematic and technical, with the confidence of a well-produced exhibition rather than a template or résumé page.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS layouts, identical card grids, glassmorphism, cream editorial templates, nightclub neon, rainbow shaders, fake metrics, skill percentages, stock imagery, heavy scroll hijacking, perpetual loading screens, bouncy motion, and effects that obscure the work.

## Design Principles

1. Let real work carry the page: imagery is evidence, not decoration.
2. Treat production detail as a creative strength through honest metadata and technical annotation.
3. Separate design and music into distinct rhythms while keeping one visual voice.
4. Make unconventional composition easy to navigate and fully keyboard-accessible.
5. Use motion and WebGL as atmosphere; core content must remain complete without either.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain logical headings, keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, meaningful image alternatives, touch-safe controls, reduced-motion behavior, no autoplaying audio, and complete non-WebGL fallbacks.
